Summer pickup lines can look informal compared with the school year, but they often combine distracted adults, excited children, bikes, heat, parking pressure, and confusing handoff rules. This guide was checked on 2026-06-20 against NHTSA, CDC, FHWA, and Safe Routes resources. It is not legal advice; obey local signs, staff directions, and law-enforcement instructions.

## Decision table

| Situation | Safer choice | Mistake to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Approaching camp pickup | Slow early and scan sidewalks, bikes, and door zones | Rolling in while reading messages |
| Line is backed up | Wait legally or circle as directed | Double parking or blocking sight lines |
| Child is not ready | Stay parked and follow staff process | Calling them across moving traffic |
| Heat or errand pressure rises | Reset the routine and check passengers | Leaving anyone in the car unattended |

## 1. Make the slow zone start before the driveway
Reduce speed before the visible congestion. Children, bikes, and late lane changes can appear before the official
entrance. A driver who waits until the last second to slow down has already lost reaction margin.

![Supporting visual 2](/images/illustrations/di-school-camp-pickup-20260620-02.png)

Practical rule: write the stop condition before you begin. If the real situation crosses that line, choose the lower-

## 2. Turn the phone into a parked-only tool
Use messages and maps before moving or after parking. Pickup zones are full of short glances that feel harmless but can
hide pedestrians, strollers, or staff signals.

## 3. Plan the handoff like a checklist
Know where the child enters, who opens doors, where bags go, and what the no-go rule is if the pickup location changes.
Predictability reduces backing, double parking, and last-second crossing.

## 4. Respect heat as a driving-safety issue
Waiting vehicles, hot seats, distracted errands, and quick stops can create risk. Never leave children or pets
unattended in vehicles, and build reminders into the handoff routine.
